Executive Summary

How I Work

I am a Senior Android Engineer with more than ten years of experience across FinTech, EdTech, HealthTech, Logistics, E-commerce and Enterprise products. I have contributed to production apps used by customers in several regions, delivering secure, scalable and maintainable mobile software in Agile teams.

My experience covers the whole lifecycle: requirements analysis and architecture, implementation, testing, deployment and production support. I enjoy hard engineering problems, modernising legacy codebases, and building software that stays maintainable as it grows.

Day to day that means Kotlin-first development, Jetpack Compose, Clean Architecture with MVVM or MVI, REST and GraphQL integrations, and CI/CD pipelines that make releases routine. I work closely with product managers, designers, backend engineers and QA, and I mentor other Android developers through code reviews and technical discussions.
